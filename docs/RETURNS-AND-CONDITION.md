## How our stock works

Marathon Club is an independent reseller. We source from clearance lines,
factory surplus and pre-loved stock, which is why quantities are limited and
most pieces are rarely restocked. Every item is checked by hand before it is
listed.

Marathon Club is not an authorised dealer for, and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by, any third-party rights holder. No manufacturer warranty applies
to anything we sell.

## Condition grades

Every listing states one of three condition grades. Items are sold as found,
with their condition disclosed up front:

- **Excellent — no visible wear.** As close to unworn as pre-loved and surplus
  stock gets. No marks we could find on hand inspection.
- **Very good — light cosmetic marks.** Minor signs of handling or storage —
  a light scuff, a faint mark — nothing that affects use.
- **Good — visible wear, priced accordingly.** Honest wear you can see, and a
  price that reflects it. What we found is disclosed; nothing is hidden.

Original packaging isn't always included, and boxes may show storage wear.

## 14-day exchange on anything faulty

If an item turns out to be faulty — a defect we missed, not the disclosed
condition or ordinary wear from use — contact us within **14 days of
delivery** and we will exchange it. If no exchange is possible we will make it
right another way.

To claim: send your order number and a photo of the fault to the contact
details on your order confirmation. Items must come back in the state you
received them, with everything that shipped with them.

What the 14-day exchange does **not** cover:

- The condition grade disclosed on the listing (that is what you bought).
- Wear caused after delivery.
- Change of mind on sizing or styling.

Nothing here limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act.
