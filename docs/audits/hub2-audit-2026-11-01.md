## What this means for "a further Hub 2 problem"

The requests are not coming from a bug. They are the scan doing what Hub 2's policy says:
keep N of every size of every armed line. When Central receives a new line, Hub 2's
policy fills every size from zero, whether or not anyone has bought it yet. That is
where most of the build-up comes from: since 24 Oct, Central sent Hub 2 **1,296 units**
and Hub 2 moved out **1,214** (416 sold, 604 to PE/Trophy, 194 elsewhere). In the week
of 26 Oct Hub 2 took in 1,241 and sent out 875 to the shops and its own till. Hub 2 holds **10,498 units**.

**Not changed (Junid's rule: policy/targets/request logic only on his specific ask).**
If Junid wants Hub 2 to stop pre-filling lines that have not sold, the levers are policy, not code:

1. the t-shirt / caps-beanies / soccer-jersey **category policies at Hub 2** (the bulk of the no-sales units), or
2. a first-fill rule ("arm a new line at Hub 2 only after its first sale at PE/Trophy").

Either is a policy change and needs his go-ahead.
