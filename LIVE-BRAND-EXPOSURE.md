# Live brand exposure — every product, every field

Scanned **703 active Shopify products**, 2026-08-22, across every field the
push writes: title, handle, vendor, product type, tags, description, SEO title,
SEO description, and image alt text. Collections were scanned too (title, handle,
description, SEO) — **clean, no hits**.

**139 products — 19.8% of the shop — carry at least one mark the
brand lexicon does not know.** 88 distinct marks, none of them recognised.

Each mark lands in **five pushed fields at once** — title, handle, SEO title, SEO
description and image alt — because all five derive from the title. One dirty
title is five exposures, and the SEO description is what Google shows under the
result.

**Nothing here has been changed.** This is the list; what comes off the shop, and
when, is yours to decide.
